# Hebrew naming: per-food exceptions

Food names are made natural by **general rules** (the naming principles in the image
prompt, `src/lib/analysis.js`, and the guard in `src/lib/hebrewName.js`), not by a list of
particular foods. Fixing one dish by hard-coding it does not help the next dish that
goes wrong, so a general rule is always preferred.

A per-food exception is allowed only when a general rule cannot express it, and then:

- it needs the **owner's approval**;
- it must state the **reason** why no general rule covers it.

## Exceptions

None. The list is empty.
